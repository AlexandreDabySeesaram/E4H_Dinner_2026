Why a lung digital twin ?

\[ \definecolor{Violet}{RGB}{162, 32, 185} \definecolor{Teal}{RGB}{0, 103, 127} \definecolor{Blue}{RGB}{58, 70, 245} \definecolor{Green}{RGB}{0,103,127} \definecolor{LGreen}{RGB}{62,128,102} \definecolor{red}{RGB}{206,0,55} \]

Objectives

  • Improved understanding of the mechanically-driven physiological mechanisms
    • e.g. fibrosis, ventilation, perfusion
  • In silico prognostics
    • Transfer tools to the clinic
    • Patient-specific digital twins

Requirements

  • Mechanical model of the lung
  • Image-based personalisation & uncertainty quantification
  • Real-time estimation of the parameters

Modelling & image-based personalisation

Lung poro-mechanics - two scales

  • Macro: finite-strain poro-mechanical model of the parenchyma (Patte et al., 2022; Peyraut & Genet, 2024)
    • Solid skeleton + air, hyperelastic potentials \(\Psi_s\left(\boldsymbol{E}, \Phi_s\right)\)
  • Micro: alveolar microstructure (Manoochehrtayebi et al., 2025)
    • Blood perfusion (Haotian Xiao)
  • Pleural pressure: data-driven reduced model (Felipe Álvarez-Barrientos)

Motion tracking & personalisation

  • Equilibrated warping (Genet et al., 2018)
    • Finite element image registration
    • Mechanically regularised (equilibrium gap)
  • Patient-specific estimation of regional compliance (Laville et al., 2023)
  • Uncertainty quantification (Peyraut & Genet, 2024)

Towards real-time digital twins

Patient-specific parametrised simulation

Surrogate modelling - NeuROM

  • Interpretable reduced-order model (hybrid FE / NN-PGD)
    • (Daby-Seesaram et al., 2025; Škardová et al., 2025)
  • Parametrised solution learned offline as a PGD approximation
    • Material, loading and patient shape parameters

\[ \boldsymbol{u}\left(\textcolor{Blue}{\boldsymbol{x}}, \textcolor{LGreen}{\left\{\mu_i\right\}_{i \in \mathopen{~[\!\![~}1, \beta \mathclose{~]\!\!]}}}\right) = \sum\limits_{i=1}^m \textcolor{Blue}{\overline{\boldsymbol{u}}_i(\boldsymbol{x})} ~\textcolor{LGreen}{\prod_{j=1}^{\beta}\lambda_i^j(\mu^j)} \]

  • On-the-fly evaluation online: real-time simulation

Enriching the digital twin

  • Micro-buckling of the alveolar walls (PhD: Solal Espic)
    • Account for alveolar collapse & step towards multi-scale DT
  • Pleural pressure model in the DT (PhD: Salvador Gonzalez)
    • State-of-the-art macro model incorporated in the DT
  • Applications: diagnostics, prognostics, control of medical devices
